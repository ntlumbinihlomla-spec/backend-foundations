# Bookkeeper Client Dashboard Outreach

## My Opinion

This is the right move.

Do not pitch this as a broad financial dashboard. Pitch it as a daily control tool for bookkeepers and accountants:

"A simple client dashboard that shows who owes what, which documents are missing, and what needs attention today."

That is easier to sell because bookkeepers already feel this pain every week. They chase clients for invoices, receipts, bank statements, payments, and missing info. The MVP should help them chase less and look more organised.

## If They Reply "What Does It Do?"

It gives the bookkeeper one place to see:

- overdue invoices
- outstanding invoice totals
- missing receipts or client documents
- client/supplier records
- audit trail
- report exports
- bank CSV import and matching

The first pilot is not a full accounting system. It is a lightweight client control dashboard for the painful follow-up work.
