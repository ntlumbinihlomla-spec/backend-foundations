# Direct Client Recovery Plan

This plan replaces marketplace dependency with direct sales for the Backend Foundations financial dashboard offer.

Current counter: Week 4, starting from Week 1 on Monday, May 11, 2026.

## Immediate Protection

Do this first if Fiverr or a buyer message looked suspicious:

1. Stop clicking links or replying to the suspicious message.
2. Screenshot the message, profile, links, payment requests, emails, and timestamps.
3. Change the Fiverr password and enable two-factor authentication.
4. Contact Fiverr Support through the official Help Center.
5. If card, bank, or payment details were shared, contact the bank or payment provider immediately.
6. Do not pay any recovery service, verification fee, release fee, or outside-platform fee to get work.

## Remote Prospect List

Do this from home. Do not visit businesses in person.

Contact these buyers in this order:

1. Bookkeepers and accountants
   - They already handle CSVs, bank exports, payroll files, sales reports, VAT reports, and management accounts.
   - Best pitch: help them give better monthly reports to their SME clients.

2. Small accounting firms
   - They can become repeat buyers because one firm may have many clients.
   - Best pitch: one dashboard/report template they can reuse across clients.

3. Business consultants and small agencies
   - They often need reporting for clients but may not build tools themselves.
   - Best pitch: white-label client report production.

4. SMEs with regular transactions
   - Retailers, salons, gyms, restaurants, e-commerce shops, property managers, clinics, repair businesses, and wholesalers.
   - Best pitch: turn sales and expense CSVs into a clean monthly owner report.

5. Nonprofits and churches with treasurers
   - They often need simple contribution and spending summaries.
   - Best pitch: clean monthly finance reports for committees.
