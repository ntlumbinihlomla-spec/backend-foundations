# Freelance Launch Plan

## Offer

I build simple financial dashboards for SMEs that turn CSV exports into clean summaries, batch analytics, and client-ready reports.

## Target Clients

Start with businesses that already export CSV files and need recurring reports:

- Bookkeepers and small accounting firms
- Small retail stores
- Local service businesses
- Freelance finance consultants
- SMEs using spreadsheets for monthly reviews
- Founders who ask someone to manually clean reports every month

## 7-Day Launch Sprint

Day 1: Prepare the Demo

- Open `frontend/freelance.html` and check your contact link.
- Open `frontend/landing.html` and confirm the demo flow.
- Prepare one clean sample CSV with an `amount` column.
- Record a 60-90 second walkthrough showing upload, batch summary, and report export.

Day 2: Build the Lead List

- List 30 local SMEs, bookkeepers, and consultants.
- Prioritize people who already know you or are one warm introduction away.
- Add their name, company, email, phone, and what you think they report on.

Day 3: Send 10 Warm Messages

- Send the short WhatsApp or email pitch.
- Offer to show a working demo, not to "talk about a product."
- Ask one simple question: "Do you review CSV or spreadsheet reports manually?"
- If you are using Fiverr, publish the Gig from `docs/fiverr-gig-setup.md` before sending messages.

Day 4: Send 20 Cold Messages

- Use LinkedIn, email, or business contact forms.
- Keep the message short.
- Mention a fixed-price pilot rather than open-ended development.

Day 5: Demo Calls

- Show the landing page first.
- Show the dashboard second.
- Upload the sample CSV.
- Export the report.
- Ask for one sample file if they want a tailored demo.

Day 6: Close the Pilot

- Offer the `CSV Report Pilot` at R3,500.
- Promise a narrow result: one CSV format, one dashboard, one report.
- Ask for a 50% deposit before build work starts.

Day 7: Follow Up

- Follow up with everyone who replied.
- Send the demo link again.
- Ask whether they want the pilot this week or next week.
- Send the Fiverr Gig link when a buyer wants a marketplace checkout path.

## Discovery Call Questions

Ask these in order:

1. What CSV or spreadsheet report do you review most often?
2. Who prepares it now?
3. How often do you need it?
4. Which numbers matter most?
5. What do you send to clients, managers, or owners afterward?
6. What makes the current process annoying?
7. Can you share a sanitized sample file?
8. If I build a pilot for one report, what would make it worth paying for?

## Pilot Scope

CSV Report Pilot:

- One uploaded CSV format
- One selected numeric column
- Summary cards
- Batch analytics if there are multiple files
- Exportable report
- Basic branding
- One walkthrough

Out of scope for the pilot:

- Accounting system integrations
- Bank connections
- Multi-user permissions
- Complex forecasting
- Data cleanup for every possible CSV format
- Long-term hosting hardening
